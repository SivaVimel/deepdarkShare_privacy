Privacy Policy

DeepDarkShare · Effective 8 November 2026

In short

  • We collect nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports sent to us.
  • There is no DeepDarkShare server. Files go directly from phone to phone through the Tor network.
  • Everything stays on your device: received files, your transfer history and your settings.
  • Transfers are end-to-end encrypted with a key that exists only in the receive code.

1Who we are

DeepDarkShare is an iPhone and iPad app for sending files anonymously from one device to another through the Tor network. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. You can reach us at sivavimelrajhen@gmail.com.

2Information we collect

None. We do not operate servers that receive your files, names, addresses or usage. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not create an account or ask for an email address, phone number or any identifier. We have no way to see who uses the app, what is shared, or with whom.

4What the other person sees

During a transfer, the person you exchange files with sees your display name (a random name such as "Violet Fox 27" unless you change it) and the names and sizes of the files. They do not see your phone number, Apple ID, IP address or location. A receiver must accept each incoming transfer after seeing the sender's display name and file list.

5How transfers work

Every connection goes through Tor. When you tap Receive, your device creates a new, one-time hidden (.onion) address and a random key, shown as a QR code, a link and a typeable code. The address is removed when you leave the Receive screen. Files are encrypted end to end with that key (ChaCha20-Poly1305), on top of Tor's own encryption, and each file's integrity is checked with SHA-256 before it is saved. Tor relays carry the encrypted data but cannot read it, and they cannot tell which two devices are communicating.

Anyone holding a receive code can try to send files to that receiver while the Receive screen is open, which is why every transfer must be accepted. A copied receive link expires from the clipboard after 10 minutes and is not synced to your other devices. Share codes only with people you trust.

Photos, videos and files you send are chosen with Apple's system pickers, so the app receives only the items you select and never asks to read your photo library. DeepDarkShare does not use location, contacts, Bluetooth, local-network discovery, microphone or tracking.

7The onion network map

The map shows the Tor relays your connection uses, placed by country with an offline database built into the app. Your own position on the map is the region from your device's language and region settings, not your location. No lookups are made to any service to draw the map.

8Third parties

The app includes the open-source Tor software and its libraries (OpenSSL, libevent, liblzma), which run entirely on your device. Connections are made only to the volunteer-run Tor network and, through it, to the other person's device. No information is shared with or sold to any third party. If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process that message; we use it only to reply.

9Children

DeepDarkShare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10Security

We designed the app to keep as little as possible: hidden addresses are generated fresh and never stored, keys are random and single-use, partial files from interrupted transfers are deleted, and the app's own logs contain no file contents or codes. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but your files are never handed to us or to any server.

11Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control everything stored by the app on your device and can delete it at any time as described in section 3.

12Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the policy shown in the app, and change the effective date above.
